# Trip planning notes

Preliminary research for upcoming trips (researched 2026-07-19). Used to pick
which Google Flights trackers to create.

## DC trip — husband + almost-5-year-old

Constraint: not hot, not freezing. DC is oppressively hot/humid June–mid Sept
(90s + humidity), freezing-ish mid Dec–Feb.

**Best windows:**
- **October 2026** — the sweet spot. Highs ~65–72°F, fall color, low humidity.
  September is also this route's cheapest fare month, so booking in Sept for
  Oct travel aligns with the cheap window.
- **Early November 2026** — still mild (upper 50s–low 60s), cheap, thin crowds.
- **Mid/late April–May 2027** — nice weather; avoid late Mar–mid Apr
  (cherry blossom crowds + hotel spikes).

**Fare notes (MSP→DC):** budget carriers put one-way fares as low as $67–100;
Sunday departures tend cheapest, Monday priciest; typical best-booking window
~5 weeks out. DCA is the close-in airport; IAD/BWI sometimes cheaper.

**Suggested trackers (round trip, 1 adult + 1 child):**
- Oct 7–11, 2026 (Wed–Sun)
- Oct 14–18, 2026 (Wed–Sun)
- Oct 21–25, 2026 (Wed–Sun)
- Nov 5–8, 2026 (Thu–Sun)
- Plus one "Any dates" tracker on MSP→Washington to catch outliers.

## Sweden trip — 2 adults + 3 kids (passports needed first)

**Passports (as of Jul 2026):** routine ~4–6 weeks processing + up to 2 weeks
mailing; expedited (+$60) ~2–3 weeks. Kids under 16 apply in person (DS-11)
with BOTH parents present, and kids' passports last only 5 years. Applying by
~August 2026 → passports comfortably in hand by fall 2026. No constraint on a
2027 trip.

**Weather vs price (Stockholm):**
- June–mid Aug: best weather (highs 68–75°F, very long days) but peak prices.
- Late May–early June: nearly as good, cheaper, fewer crowds — top pick.
- Mid/late Aug–early Sept: warm start, crayfish-party season, prices easing.
- Nov & Feb are the cheapest fare months (~$424–491 RT) but dark/cold — not a
  fit for a family sightseeing trip.

**Fare notes (MSP→ARN/STO):** typical RT $452–839 depending on season; ×5
travelers, so a $150/ticket dip ≈ $750 saved. International sweet spot for
booking is roughly 2–8 months out.

**Suggested plan:** target **late May–mid June 2027** (primary) with
**Aug 7–24, 2027** as the alternate window. Google Flights only opens dates
~11 months out, so June 2027 dates become trackable around Aug–Sept 2026 —
set trackers then, e.g.:
- May 29 – Jun 8, 2027
- Jun 5 – 15, 2027
- Aug 7 – 17, 2027 (alternate)
- Plus an "Any dates" tracker on MSP→Stockholm now for baseline data.
